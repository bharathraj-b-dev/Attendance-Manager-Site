Attendance at a glance

Your overall percentage sits at the top of the home screen, with present, absent and marked counts. Every subject gets its own status — safe, warning or critical — and the next class is always one tap away.

Theory & Practical, kept separate

A subject can hold both lectures and labs. Switch views to see each on its own.

Overall combines Theory and Practical sessions into one percentage.

When a class is over, record what actually happened.

Mark any class as Attend, Bunk or OD (On Duty). Classes that have passed without an update show up as pending, so nothing slips through.

Try them.

Before you skip that class, check the math.

The What-If Calculator works out how many classes you can miss and still stay above your target, or how many you need to attend to reach it. It's plain arithmetic on your numbers — no guesswork.

Class reminders

Get a notification before a class, and mark Attend, Bunk or OD straight from it. Reminders are local and can be switched off in Settings, along with your target (default 75%) and theme.

Already have attendance in DigiCampus?

Import it instead of starting from zero. Sign in to DigiCampus inside the app, open your attendance records, and tap Import. Optional — you can also just track manually.

Your attendance stays with you.

AttendanceManager stores its attendance data on your Android device. There are no online accounts and no cloud sync. DigiCampus import, if you use it, loads DigiCampus pages in the app so you sign in there directly.

Questions

Is AttendanceManager free?

Yes. Download it from GitHub Releases.

Is my attendance stored online?

No. Attendance data is stored locally on your device. There are no accounts or cloud sync.

Do I need a DigiCampus account?

Only if you want to import from DigiCampus. You can add and track attendance manually without one.

Is AttendanceManager officially affiliated with DigiCampus?

No. It's an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by DigiCampus or your institution.

How do I update AttendanceManager?

Download the newer APK from GitHub Releases and install it over the existing app. Don't uninstall first.

Will updating delete my attendance?

No, updating over the existing app keeps your local data. Uninstalling may remove it, so avoid that during normal updates.

Where can I report a bug?

Open an issue on the GitHub issues page.
